Introducing Copper—a candle crafted with intention rather than impulse.

This composition balances dry spice, resinous woods, tobacco, and smoke into a sophisticated blend that feels simultaneously warm and restrained.

As it burns, vanilla and musk emerge, softening the base into a refined, room-filling fragrance that promotes mental clarity and calm. Hand-poured in Sweden from 100% natural soy wax, Copper delivers a measured burn that maximizes longevity and scent distribution.

The slow-burning formula ensures consistent, gentle diffusion throughout your space—a deliberate choice for those who value substance over spectacle. This is a candle designed to anchor your environment with understated presence and grounded warmth.
